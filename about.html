About & Engineering Methodology (사이트 소개 및 산출 원칙)

Welcome to the Precision Consumer Utility Index (kbu3986.github.io). We build fast, bilingual (English & Korean) calculation tools that turn opaque regulatory tables, tax formulas, and nutrition labels into clear, actionable numbers.

1. WattCheck — Aviation Battery Wh & DC-DC Efficiency Model

  • Core Formula: Wh = (mAh × Nominal Voltage V) ÷ 1,000, aligned with IATA Dangerous Goods Regulations (2.3.5.9), US FAA PackSafe, and South Korea MOLIT 2025–2026 cabin safety rules.
  • Real-World Usable Capacity: Accounts for DC-DC voltage conversion heat losses (default 82% net efficiency when stepping 3.7V cells up to 5V/9V/15V/20V USB-C PD).

2. WorthMove — 2026 Net Take-Home & True Hourly Wage Engine

  • Global & US Tax Model: Incorporates 2026 standard deductions, progressive federal brackets, FICA (Social Security wage-base cap + Medicare), and state/country presets.
  • Korean Statutory Payroll Model (2026): Computes National Pension (국민연금), Health Insurance (건강보험), Long-Term Care (장기요양보험), Employment Insurance (고용보험), and progressive Earned Income + Local Income Tax after non-taxable meal allowances.
  • True Hourly Formula: Divides annual net pay minus annual commute out-of-pocket expenses by total annual hours committed (work hours + round-trip commute hours + unpaid overtime).

3. PureGram — Active Ingredient Purity & Cost-Per-Gram Audit

  • Purity Ratio: (Pure Active Grams per Serving ÷ Total Serving Weight) × 100, exposing non-active excipients, gums, maltodextrin, or non-EPA/DHA carrier oils.
  • Omega-3 Standard: Evaluates strictly by combined pure EPA + DHA (mg) per softgel—never raw fish oil weight.
