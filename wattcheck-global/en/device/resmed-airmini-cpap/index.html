ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night) Battery & Travel FAQ

Q. How many Watt-hours (Wh) is the ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night) battery?

The ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night) battery holds 56 Wh (15,135 mAh at 24V nominal voltage), sitting well below the 100 Wh airline carry-on limit.

Q. How many times will a 20,000 mAh power bank charge ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night)?

A 20,000 mAh power bank (74 Wh rated, ~60.68 Wh net usable at 82% DC-DC efficiency) charges ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night) (56 Wh) approximately 1.08 times from 0% to 100%. A 10,000 mAh pack delivers ~0.54 charges, and a 27,000 mAh TSA-max pack delivers ~1.46 charges.

Q. What wattage USB-C power bank does ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night) need?

ResMed AirMini Travel CPAP (8h Night) supports up to 65W fast charging (20V USB-C PD (65W+) via 24V DC converter cable (waterless HumidX)). Choose a power bank rated for at least 65W USB-C PD output.
