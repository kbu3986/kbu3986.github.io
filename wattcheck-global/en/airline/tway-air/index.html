Mandatory Korean Airline Power Bank Cabin Rules

Enforced since March 2025 across Korean Air, Asiana, Jeju Air, Jin Air, T'way, Air Premia, Air Busan, and flights departing Korean airports:

1. Overhead Bin Storage STRICTLY BANNED

Power banks and e-cigarettes must NEVER be placed inside overhead luggage bins. Keep them on your person or inside the seat-back pocket in front of you.

2. Terminal Insulation Tape OR Clear Zip-Lock Pouch

Every power bank must have exposed USB ports covered with insulating tape, port caps, OR be stored individually inside a transparent zip-lock plastic bag / protective pouch.

3. In-Flight Power Bank Charging Ban

Charging a power bank using the aircraft's in-seat USB/AC port is prohibited, and Korean carriers prohibit using power banks to charge devices mid-flight—verify crew announcements.

4. Quantity & Sticker Tier (≤100Wh: Max 5 pcs / 100–160Wh: Max 2 pcs)

Up to 5 units ≤100Wh allowed per passenger without approval; 100.1–160Wh capped at 2 units with mandatory check-in counter approval stickers. Unlabeled batteries are refused.

T'way Air Battery FAQ

Q. Can I bring a 20,000 mAh power bank on T'way Air?

Yes. A 20,000 mAh power bank rated at 3.7V equals 74 Wh, which is under T'way Air's 100 Wh carry-on threshold (up to 5 piece(s) without approval). Never place it in checked luggage.

Q. Does T'way Air allow power banks in overhead bins or in-flight charging?

Overhead bin storage: STRICTLY BANNED — keep in your seat pocket or under-seat personal bag. In-flight charging/discharging: STRICTLY PROHIBITED throughout the flight.

Q. Are 100.1–160 Wh power banks allowed on T'way Air?

Yes, up to 2 units between 100.1 Wh and 160 Wh are permitted in cabin baggage ONLY with explicit T'way Air check-in counter approval. Batteries exceeding 160 Wh are banned.
